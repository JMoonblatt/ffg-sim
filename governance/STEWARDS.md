# Rotating Stewardship

Stewardship is procedural. It is not leadership.

## Steward powers (only)
- Enforce the process (proposal + decision log + metric + rollback)
- Break ties after a documented deadlock
- Ensure decisions are logged

## Steward non-powers
- Cannot unilaterally set direction
- Cannot approve their own proposal without at least 1 reviewer
- Cannot serve two rotations in a row

## Rotation
- Rotation length: 7–14 days (project chooses one)
- Next steward is predetermined (round-robin list or random shuffle)
- If a steward disappears, the next steward auto-inherits immediately

## Deadlock rule
If a proposal cannot reach a decision after 72 hours:
1) Steward chooses one option as a time-boxed experiment, OR
2) Apply FORK.md if disagreement persists
